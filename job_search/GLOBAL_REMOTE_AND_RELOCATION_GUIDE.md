# Global remote and relocation guide

This is the decision guide for international opportunities. It is not a tax or legal opinion; confirm a specific
offer, residency position, and contractor setup with a qualified CA or immigration adviser before signing.

## Objective

Current compensation is ₹30L cash plus separate benefits at four years of experience. A move should materially
improve at least one of: cash savings, remote flexibility, quality of life/perks, or international relocation.

Use four search lanes:

| Lane | Search share | Main outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Premium India / India-remote | 30% | Reliable ₹40L+ cash and better role quality |
| UAE relocation | 30% | High savings and international move |
| Foreign remote from India | 25% | Currency upside while retaining Indian cost base |
| Germany / Netherlands | 15% | Long-term quality of life and EU residency path |

## Offer filters

These are personal search filters, not market guarantees.

- **India:** prioritize ₹40L+ cash. Consider a lower amount only for an unusually strong remote, brand, equity, or
  quality-of-life trade-off.
- **UAE:** engage seriously at AED 25k+ monthly cash with visa and medical insurance; AED 30k+ is the strong
  target. Usually skip below AED 22k unless the non-cash upside is exceptional.
- **Foreign remote contractor:** seek at least about ₹50L / US$60k equivalent before accepting contractor risk,
  compliance, and possible non-India working hours.
- **Foreign remote through EOR:** target roughly ₹45L+ equivalent plus meaningful employment benefits.
- **Germany / Netherlands:** salary merely meeting immigration minimums is usually a lifestyle/long-term-residency
  move, not a savings-maximizing move.

Always compare cash, bonus, equity, insurance, leave, working hours, notice period, relocation support, and actual
living costs; do not compare headline CTC alone.

## Foreign remote: eligibility first

"Remote" alone does not mean a company can hire in India. Before spending substantial interview time, ask:

> Can you hire an employee residing in India? Would the arrangement be through your Indian entity/EOR or as an
> independent contractor? Is India an approved permanent work location for this role?

### EOR versus independent contractor

- **EOR or direct Indian employment:** the company employs you locally or through an employer of record. Payroll,
  statutory compliance, and benefits are simpler; compensation can be lower.
- **Independent contractor:** you invoice the foreign company and generally receive more cash, but must handle tax,
  compliance, insurance, leave, termination risk, and payment mechanics yourself.

## India tax and GST: validate before accepting a contract

- Do not treat presumptive taxation under Section 44ADA as a loophole. It applies only when the legal conditions,
  profession classification, and receipt rules are actually met.
- The current ITR-4 guidance describes a ₹75L threshold only where cash receipts are at most 5% of total receipts;
  otherwise the relevant threshold is ₹50L. A high foreign-contract income may exceed it.
- Export of qualifying services can be zero-rated for GST, but registration, LUT/bond, invoicing, FEMA/payment flow,
  and your exact service classification require professional advice.
- Speak to a CA before choosing contractor versus EOR employment, not after the first payment arrives.

References: [Income Tax ITR-4 FAQ](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/e-filing-itr4-form-sugam-faq),
[Section 44ADA](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-44ada-9), and
[GST export-of-services FAQ](https://goagst.gov.in/PDF/FAQ/IT_English.pdf).

## UAE: savings-first relocation lane

- UAE has no personal income tax. Employers sponsor normal work permits and generally manage the work-visa process.
- UAE private-sector employees receive 30 paid annual-leave days after one year. Dubai employers provide employee
  health insurance.
- Rent is the deciding cost. Compare a real offer against likely housing, transport, and lifestyle costs before
  moving; do not assume a tax-free salary automatically produces large savings.

References: [UAE tax guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation),
[work permits](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/job-offers-and-work-permits-and-contracts/work-permits),
[annual leave](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/types-of-leaves-and-entitlements-in-the-private-sector/annual-leave),
and [Dubai rental data](https://datadubai.ae/rents/).

## Germany and Netherlands: residency-first lanes

- **Germany:** pursue roles with salary sufficient for an EU Blue Card. The 2026 standard threshold is €50,700;
  the lower shortage/IT threshold is €45,934.20 subject to the route's conditions.
- **Netherlands:** apply only to employers recognized by the IND and offering sponsorship. Under-30 highly skilled
  migrants need at least €4,357 monthly in 2026.
- The Dutch 30% facility is useful but policy is subject to change; verify the rule applying when an offer is made.

References: [Germany EU Blue Card](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card),
[Netherlands highly skilled migrant route](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant), and
[Dutch income requirements](https://ind.nl/en/required-amounts-income-requirements).

## Avoid

- US-only, EU-only, or vague "remote" roles that do not include India.
- Low-paid foreign contractor roles that merely replace benefits with a flashy currency label.
- UAE offers without written visa, insurance, leave, notice, and compensation terms.
- Europe roles without sponsorship or with salary that cannot support the intended lifestyle.
- Recruiters who request money, conceal the employer, or will not clarify engagement type.
