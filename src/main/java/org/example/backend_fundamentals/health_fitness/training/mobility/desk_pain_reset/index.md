# Anytime Desk-Pain Reset

Use this whenever sitting causes lower back pain, upper neck pain, or rounded shoulders. Keep it gentle and slow.

| Step | Movement | Dose | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Stand and walk | 1-2 min | unload back and hips |
| 2 | Chin tucks / double-chin tuck | 8-10 reps | forward-head posture, deep neck flexors |
| 3 | Neck rotations | 5/side | neck stiffness |
| 4 | Levator scapulae stretch | 20 sec/side | upper neck, shoulder corner |
| 5 | Shoulder rolls back and down | 10 reps | neck/shoulder tension |
| 6 | Scapular retractions | 12 reps | rounded shoulders |
| 7 | Doorway chest stretch | 30 sec | tight pecs |
| 8 | Thoracic extension on chair | 8 reps | upper back posture |
| 9 | Cat cow | 8 reps | spine movement |
| 10 | Posterior pelvic tilts | 8-10 reps | lower-back position, core control |
| 11 | Knee-to-chest stretch | 20 sec/side | lower back, glutes |
| 12 | Glute bridge | 10 reps | glutes, lower-back support |
| 13 | Bird dog | 6/side | core, hips, lower-back posture |

Stop if pain increases, shoots down the arm/leg, or causes numbness/tingling.
