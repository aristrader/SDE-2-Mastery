# Cool-Down

Do this after the walk/run + workout block.

| Step | Exercise | Time / reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Slow walk | 5 min |
| 2 | Standing calf stretch | 30 sec each side |
| 3 | Standing quad stretch | 30 sec each side |
| 4 | Hamstring stretch | 30 sec each side |
| 5 | Figure-4 glute stretch | 30 sec each side |
| 6 | Hip flexor stretch | 30 sec each side |
| 7 | Slow breathing | 1 min |

Keep stretches gentle. Do not force painful ranges.
