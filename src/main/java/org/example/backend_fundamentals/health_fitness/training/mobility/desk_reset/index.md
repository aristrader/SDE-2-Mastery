# Desk Reset

Do this every 45-60 min during office work.

| Step | Movement | Dose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Stand and walk | 1-2 min |
| 2 | Chin tucks / double-chin tuck | 5 reps |
| 3 | Shoulder rolls back and down | 5 reps |
| 4 | Scapular retractions | 5 reps |
| 5 | Chest opener stretch | 15-20 sec |

Keep the neck relaxed. Chin tuck means sliding the head straight back, not looking down.
