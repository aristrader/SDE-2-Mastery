# Recovery Workout

Use Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday.

| Order | Movement | Muscles | Sets x reps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sit-to-stand | Quads, glutes | 2 x 10 |
| 2 | Wall pushup | Chest, shoulders, triceps | 2 x 10 |
| 3 | Band/backpack row | Upper back, biceps | 2 x 10 |
| 4 | Glute bridge | Glutes, hamstrings | 2 x 12 |
| 5 | Bird dog | Core, spinal control, glutes | 2 x 8/side |
| 6 | Dead bug | Deep core | 2 x 8/side |
| 7 | Farmer carry | Grip, traps, core, posture | 2 x 30 sec |

Keep this easy. No grinding.
