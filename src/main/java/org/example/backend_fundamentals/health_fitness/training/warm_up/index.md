# Warm-Up

Do this before the walk/run block.

| Step | Exercise | Time / reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Easy walk | 3-5 min |
| 2 | Ankle circles | 10 each side |
| 3 | Leg swings front/back | 10 each leg |
| 4 | Leg swings side/side | 10 each leg |
| 5 | Walking lunges or reverse lunges | 8 each leg |
| 6 | High knees or marching high knees | 30 sec |
| 7 | Butt kicks or easy heel flicks | 30 sec |

Start the run easy for the first few minutes. The goal is to raise body temperature and loosen ankles, hips, calves, hamstrings, and glutes.
