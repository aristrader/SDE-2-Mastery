### Status emoji legend

- ☐ — not started yet
- ✅ — on track (Spent within ±2 hrs of Budget midpoint; Done rows in line with expected pace)
- 🚀 — ahead (Spent ≤ Budget − 3 hrs, or Done rows exceed expected pace)
- ⚠️ — behind (Spent ≥ Budget + 3 hrs, OR <50% of expected Done rows for this Part)
- 🚨 — way behind (current week's Parts not started by mid-week, or 2+ weeks behind cumulative)

### Drift rules — what to do if off pace

- **1 week behind:** defer one light-touch Part (14, 21, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30) entirely to Consolidation. You gain back ~2 hrs.
- **2 weeks behind:** cut a planned Part from Sprint — pick the lowest-priority one for your goals. Move to Consolidation Month 1.
- **3+ weeks behind:** reassess. Either extend Sprint by 2-4 weeks OR accept reduced Sprint scope (~70% of Parts covered, finish the rest in Consolidation).
- **Ahead by 1 week:** promote a 🟠 HIGH row from one of the deferred Parts into the current week. OR get ahead on a Mastery candidate.
- **Ahead by 2+ weeks:** you're surveying too superficially. Either deep-dive a Mastery candidate, or do an extra LLD/HLD mock.

### Off-hour signal — when one week is spent too high or too low

- **Spent > Budget + 5 hrs for any single week:** you're over-investing on familiar material. Tick `Done` more aggressively; skim instead of study.
- **Spent < Budget - 5 hrs:** you skipped the week (life happened) OR you're under-engaging. If skipped, roll content into next week's overflow Sunday. If under-engaging, that's a flag — check if the Part feels too easy and you should be deep-diving instead.

## Weekly rhythm (applies to every week)

- **Mon-Thu (~5-6 hrs):** work the current Part's 🔴 rows. Skim familiar rows, deep-dive weak ones.
- **Fri (30 min):** Friday recall slot — see table below.
- **Sat (~2-2.5 hrs, from Week 4):** one practice problem from `reference/PracticeProblems.md`. Timed, out loud, verbalize the design while you build it.
- **Sun:** explicit slack — overflow from earlier days, or revisit a `Visit Again` row.
- **Total:** ~9 hrs structured + 1-3 hrs flex = 10-12 hrs/week.
