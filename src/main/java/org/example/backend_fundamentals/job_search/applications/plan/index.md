# Application Plan

Target: Backend Engineer II, Senior Backend Engineer, and Platform Engineer roles in India or remote from India,
with ₹40L+ total compensation.

## Application system

- [ ] Set up Simplify as the application system.

## Portals and alerts

### India: primary, check daily

- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) — direct company roles, recruiter outreach, India, UAE,
  Europe, and explicitly India-eligible remote jobs.
- [Instahyre](https://www.instahyre.com/) — premium India roles and recruiter-led opportunities.
- [Cutshort](https://cutshort.io/) — India startup and remote roles; apply only when compensation and role
  quality clear the target.
- [Naukri](https://www.naukri.com/) — broad India pipeline; use seniority, salary, and remote filters.

### Remote: check two or three times weekly

- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs/) — startup roles; verify that India is an eligible work location.
- [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app/) — use India or Worldwide filters with backend, Java, Spring, or
  platform keywords.
- [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) — YC startup roles and a reusable candidate profile.
- [Arc](https://arc.dev/en-in/remote-jobs) — worldwide, India, and contract opportunities.
- [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) and [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) — useful leads,
  but only pursue roles that explicitly permit India or worldwide hiring.
- [Turing](https://www.turing.com/jobs) — secondary matching pipeline.
- [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/talent/apply) — apply after interview preparation is strong; it is a
  selective freelance network, not a guaranteed job board.

### UAE: check two or three times weekly

- [GulfTalent](https://www.gulftalent.com/UAE/jobs/)
- [Naukrigulf](https://www.naukrigulf.com/)
- LinkedIn UAE searches and direct careers pages for product, fintech, travel, bank, and KYC/fraud companies.

### Germany and Netherlands: check weekly

- [Relocate.me](https://relocate.me/)
- LinkedIn searches for `visa sponsorship`, `EU Blue Card`, `relocation`, or `highly skilled migrant`.
- Direct company careers pages. Apply only when sponsorship or international hiring is explicit.

## Application routine

Use **60–90 minutes on five weekdays**. Do not force applications when no strong role is available.

- 20–30 minutes: review fresh roles and save only eligible, high-fit positions.
- 30–45 minutes: submit two to four applications or send referral/recruiter messages.
- 5–10 minutes: update Simplify with the application, stage, compensation, and next action.

Use one 2–3 hour weekend session for UAE, global-remote, direct-company, and sponsorship searches.

### Role filter

Prioritize Backend Engineer II, Senior Backend Engineer, Software Engineer — Backend, and Platform Engineer
roles that use Java, Spring Boot, Kafka, Redis, AWS, APIs, integrations, reliability, or fraud/identity/fintech
experience. Apply only when location eligibility is clear and the role is a credible improvement over the current
position.

For foreign-remote roles, confirm India eligibility and whether the engagement is through an employer of record
(EOR) or an independent-contractor agreement before investing deeply in the interview process. See the private
`job_search/GLOBAL_REMOTE_AND_RELOCATION_GUIDE.md` for the offer, tax, and relocation rules.
